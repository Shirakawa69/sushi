AgosSink

AgosSink is a smart sink that utilizes automated waste separation technology to sort food waste, oil waste, and contaminated water, making waste disposal easier, cleaner, and more sustainable.

How Does it Work?

How does your product work?

1. You dump all the waste

Food scraps, oil, and water go down together.

2. Smart Sensors Detect

Sensors identify the type of waste by density and composition.

3. Automatic Separation

The system uses filters, separators, and channels to divide the waste.

4. Sorted Waste Storage

Each type of waste is stored in a separate, sealed compartment.

What physics principles are applied in this design?

It relies on Archimedes' Principle for buoyancy separation, Fluid Dynamics for smooth drainage flow, and Gravitational Force for multi-stage filtration.

How often do the waste compartments need to be cleaned?

The solid food basket requires daily emptying for residential use, while the dedicated oil trap container can be checked and cleaned weekly.

How does this design prevent the pipe leaks?

It captures debris at the sink level before it enters the plumbing, preventing the blockages that cause high pressure, backup, and joint failure.

Can this sink be installed in standard kitchens?

Yes, the exit drainage valves are engineered to connect directly to standard residential and commercial PVC pipes without modifying existing household plumbing.
