Sorting system // 3 streams

Three bins, zero guesswork

Each compartment is color-matched to a waste stream, so the whole household sorts on autopilot.

STREAM 01

Blue — Paper

  • Newspapers & magazines
  • Envelopes & mail
  • Flat-folded cardboard
  • Printer & notebook paper
STREAM 02

Yellow — Packaging

  • Plastic bottles & films
  • Yogurt cups & trays
  • Cans, lids & foil
  • Beverage cartons
STREAM 03

Green — Organics

  • Veggie & fruit peelings
  • Coffee grounds & filters
  • Eggshells
  • Tea bags & food scraps

SINK & DRAIN MODULEWash above, sort below

Rinse packaging at the faucet before it drops a single shelf down. The printed drain-pipe Y-bridge routes grey water around the compartments instead of through them, so every bin stays dry, pull-out clear and odor-free.
